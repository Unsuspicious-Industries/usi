KARL assembles a corpus of revolutionary press and archives to train a writing model on a base of Marxist classics in several languages. The unit is the article or the whole chapter, never an arbitrary chunk. The political line is set in post-training, not in the pretraining data.

Supervised pairs are built backwards. The response targets are published texts, and only the question that elicits each text is generated.
