trame is a transport and execution layer. It defines one contract for launches, deployments, frames, lanes, processes and steps, and picks its implementation when the crate is built. The choices are a single process with no transport, an MPI backend, or one CUDA warp per rank.

Because the choice is a Cargo feature, callers and verification fixtures are written once against the same functions, macros and bounds. The device backend is opt-in, so an ordinary build links no CUDA stack.
