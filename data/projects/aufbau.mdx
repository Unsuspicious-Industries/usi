Agents forget the shape of a tool call, mistake the type of an in-context variable, or touch data they may not. AUFBAU rules that out while the model decodes.

Token by token, it builds continuations that keep a partial syntax tree well-formed and well-typed. The constraints are attributes you define on a context-free surface. They range from type constraints to relational rules between objects.

You give the model hard rules, and it cannot break them.
