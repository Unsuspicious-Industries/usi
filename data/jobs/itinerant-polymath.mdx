Our projects cross disciplines and may not have an established method. We are looking for someone who can understand an unfamiliar problem, develop experiments and propose methods. Some projects concern questions that are difficult to describe in conventional terms.

### What you will do
- Assess ambiguous problems and propose experiments, methods and protocols.
- Work with all departments and connect ethics, profit, the future and the past.
- Document methods, failed experiments and unresolved questions.
- Maintain 24/7 contact with the team.

### You might be
- A mathematician whose work challenges conventional research.
- A philosopher who knows formal logic and uses NixOS.
- A generalist interested in stamps, patents and unusual experiences.

If others describe you as an ambitious outsider, consider applying.
